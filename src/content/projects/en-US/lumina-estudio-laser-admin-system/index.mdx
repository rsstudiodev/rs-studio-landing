**How do you replace an Excel-based administrative process with a platform designed to grow alongside the business?** That was the challenge after the launch of the Lumina Estudio Láser GDL website.

We built a **custom web application** to centralize the management of clients, records, sessions, treatments, promotions, and reports. The solution removed the dependency on multiple spreadsheets, simplified administrative tasks, and made each patient's **complete history** accessible from a single platform.

This project reflects our approach as developers: _understanding the business's processes and turning them into tools that improve **efficiency** and enable growth._
