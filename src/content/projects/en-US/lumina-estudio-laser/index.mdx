**How do I grow my business's digital presence?** That was the question the owner of Lumina Estudio Láser GDL wanted to answer when we started working together.

The business relied mainly on social media and word of mouth, so it needed a digital presence that would **convey trust**, explain its services clearly, and make it easier to attract new clients.

We built a website with a modern design, a **mobile-first** approach, **SEO** optimization, and **high performance**. The experience was designed to resolve users' most common questions and guide them naturally toward getting in touch through strategic calls to action.

This project reflects our approach as a developer: _building solutions that not only look good, but answer **real business goals** and deliver results._
